The Difference That Matters Most

K-12 Learning Studio

  • Take a Vector course such as Bloodborne Pathogens and edit it so it teaches Sachem's exposure control procedures, not a generic version. Add specific direction for custodial staff, nurses, and aides.
  • Turn a district document or PDF into a multi-module course with narration, imagery, and transcripts.
  • Add your own sections and knowledge checks anywhere in a course, then see time spent per section and question-level scores rather than a single completion flag.
  • Vector continues to push federal and state compliance updates into the courses you have edited, so customization does not create a maintenance burden for your team.
